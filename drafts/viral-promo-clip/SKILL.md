# Viral / Judge-Impressing Promo Clip Scripts

A method for scripting short-form video that (a) survives the first 3 seconds, (b) tells a tiny story, and (c) maps every beat to how it will be judged. Works for TikTok/Reels/Shorts product promos, UGC, and competition entries.

## Step 0 — Nail the brief before writing a single shot
Pin these; they change everything downstream:
- **Length** (15 / 30 / 45–60s) and **platform** (TikTok/Reels/Shorts → vertical 9:16).
- **Scoring criteria** if it's a contest (e.g. "Key Message 5% + Creativity 5% + Storytelling 5%"). Write these down — you will map each beat back to them.
- **Mandatory elements & rules** (must show product, must eat on camera, required hashtags, "Public" setting, banned content). List them and tick each off in the script.
- **The one message** the viewer must remember. One, not five.

## Step 1 — The 3-second Triple Hook (most important line in the script)
Retention in the first 3s decides distribution: clips holding ~70–85% get materially more reach; below ~40–60% get suppressed. So over-invest here.
- Stack **three hooks at once**: a **visual** hook (something eye-catching in frame 1), a **text** overlay (bold on-screen claim/question), and a **sound** hook (voice/SFX/music that matches energy).
- Keep spoken words **≤ ~12** in the first 3 seconds.
- Every hook must fire ≥1 of four triggers: **curiosity, pattern-interrupt, self-relevance, emotional arousal** (the best stack two).
- Hook archetypes: *bold statement*, *question/info-gap*, *pattern interrupt* (unexpected visual/sound), *proof-first* (lead with the result). Offer 2–3 hook options so the team can pick/test.
- The hook must connect to the payoff — no bait-and-switch.

## Step 2 — Structure as a micro hero's-journey
Even a 45s clip lands harder with a tiny arc (conflict → resolution). Judges reward story over a feature list.
```
[0–3s]   HOOK        stop the scroll (triple hook)
[3–8s]   SETUP       a relatable problem the viewer feels
[8–35s]  BUILD       rising action; show the process (sensory/ASMR); pace fast
   └─ insert a PATTERN INTERRUPT right before the climax (a beat of silence, a "…still missing something")
[climax] THE TURN    the product arrives as the "hero" that resolves the problem — the emotional peak
[..–~48] PAYOFF      show the result + reaction; prove the key message by SHOWING not telling
[..–end] CTA/BRAND   product hero shot + the one message + required hashtags
```
Look for a **creative device** unique to this brief (a pun on the brand/team/product name, a running gag, a transformation) and put it at the climax. That device is usually where the "Creativity" points live.

## Step 3 — Show, don't tell (this is where Key Message points live)
- Turn every claim into an action: don't say "not messy" — show them eating while typing/gaming with clean hands. Don't say "cheap" — show the receipt.
- Keep claims **accurate and non-exaggerated** (health/efficacy over-claims often auto-disqualify and always erode trust).
- For food: follow the **real chronological flow** (buy → cook → plate → eat); authenticity beats over-polished for Gen Z.

## Step 4 — Map every beat back to the scoring criteria
Make a small table: each criterion → the exact shot/line that earns it. If a criterion has no shot, you have a gap. This is the single highest-leverage quality check for contest clips.

## Step 5 — Production notes that decide "pro vs amateur"
- **Audio first.** Clean, close ASMR/voice with an external/clip mic — judges detect bad audio instantly. Sound design (a beat of silence → boom on the hero moment) sells the climax.
- **Light the subject** (window light / ring light); make steam, sauce, texture pop.
- **Vertical 9:16**, full-frame; multiple angles (top-down for process, macro for the hero moment).
- **Cut fast during build, slow on the climax.** Slightly raw/authentic > sterile.

## Step 6 — Deliverables to hand back
1. Goal + a criteria-mapping table.
2. 2–3 hook options (visual/text/sound each).
3. A numbered **shot list** (time · visual · sound/ASMR · on-screen text).
4. Caption + hashtags (flag the mandatory ones).
5. Production checklist + a final rules/criteria checklist.
Note when you cannot produce the actual video file — deliver the script/storyboard and say so.

| 1 | 0–3s | triple-hook frame | hook sound | bold hook (≤12 words) |
| … | | | | |
| n | –end | product hero shot | payoff music | one message + #hashtags |

## Guardrails
- One message per clip; resist cramming.
- Hook promise must be paid off.
- Respect all contest rules and keep claims truthful.
- Match tone to the audience (Gen Z: playful, authentic, meme-literate — not corporate).
